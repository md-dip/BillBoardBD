# Getting in touch, and where things live on the site

This covers the questions that have a real answer but no database row behind them:
how to reach the BillboardBD team, who decides what, and which page to go to for a
given task.

## Contacting the BillboardBD team

The team is reachable by email, and the current address is published in the platform's
own settings and shown in the footer at the bottom of every page. A phone number appears
there too when the platform has published one.

The address itself is deliberately not written into this guide. Contact details change,
and a support address copied into documentation goes stale quietly - people write to an
address nobody reads and assume they were ignored. The live values are the ones to quote,
exactly as the platform reports them.

Email is the better channel for anything that needs a record: a disputed payout, a
listing rejected for a reason that looks wrong, a refund that has not arrived, or a
request for different commercial terms.

## Who decides what

Some things simply are not the assistant's or the owner's to decide, and knowing which
saves a round trip.

**The BillboardBD team decides:** the platform commission rate, the advance percentage,
the board listing fee, how long a hold lasts, the final payment window, whether a listing
is approved, whether a booking passes review, whether proof of installation is accepted,
and when payout runs happen. Any request for a discount, a reduced commission, a waived
listing fee, or special pricing goes to the team by email — nobody else can grant it.

**The billboard owner decides:** their own board prices, whether to accept or decline a
booking request, and their payout details.

**The advertiser decides:** which board and dates to book, and what the campaign is.

## Where owners do things

- **Dashboard** — the overview, with the revenue tile and what needs attention.
- **My Billboards** — add a board, edit prices, update permit expiry dates.
- **Booking Requests** — accept or decline requests, and upload proof of installation.
  The "Awaiting Admin" tab is requests whose proof the team has not verified yet.
- **Payouts** — payout history, receipts, and the outstanding balance. Payout details
  (method, account name, account number, bank, branch) are edited here too.
- **How it works** — the full owner guide.

## Where advertisers do things

- **Find Billboards** — the map and the filters: area, type, size, price, radius.
- A board's own page — its details, and where a booking starts.
- **My bookings** — every booking, its current stage, what is still owed, and its
  invoices.
- **How it works** — the full advertiser guide.

## What the assistant can and cannot do

It can look up anything belonging to the account you are signed in with, explain how the
platform works, and tell you which page to go to.

It cannot make, change or cancel a booking, accept or decline a request, upload a proof,
move money, or change a price — those all happen on their own pages. It also cannot see
another account's records, and it cannot approve anything: a discount, a fee waiver or an
exception has to go to the team by email.
