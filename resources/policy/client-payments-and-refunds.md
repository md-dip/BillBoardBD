# Payments, invoices and refunds for advertisers

## The two payments

Every booking is paid in two parts. The advance is paid up front to submit the request;
the balance is paid after both approvals are in, before the due date set at confirmation.

The advance is a percentage of the booking total set by the BillboardBD team. The balance
is simply whatever is left.

## Why an advance is required at all

The advance is what separates a serious request from a browsing session. It reserves the
dates through two rounds of review, during which nobody else can book them — and that
only works if the slot is genuinely committed.

It is fully refundable right up until both approvals are in, which is exactly the period
where the advertiser has no control over the outcome.

## When money is refunded

The advance is refunded in full, automatically, if either the BillboardBD team or the
billboard owner declines the request. There is no form to fill in and no waiting on a
decision; the refund is issued as part of the rejection itself.

Once a booking has cleared both approvals, it is confirmed, and the advance is no longer
refundable — the dates have been taken off the market and the owner has committed the
board.

## Missing the balance due date

The due date for the balance is set when the owner accepts the booking. The window is a
fixed number of days decided by the BillboardBD team. A booking whose balance goes unpaid
does not proceed to installation.

## Invoices

Two invoices are generated per booking: one when the advance is paid, and one when the
balance is paid. Both are available from the booking itself.

The commission rate on an invoice is frozen at the moment that payment was made. If the
BillboardBD team later changes the platform commission, older bookings keep the rate they
were sold at — an invoice never changes retrospectively.

An advertiser's invoice shows what they paid. It does not break out how the money is
split between the platform and the billboard owner; that split is between BillboardBD and
the owner.

## Paying

Payments go through SSLCommerz, which supports cards and mobile financial services. The
payment page is hosted by SSLCommerz — BillboardBD never sees or stores card details.

If a payment is interrupted, nothing is lost: the booking stays where it was and the
payment can be attempted again.
