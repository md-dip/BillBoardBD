# How BillboardBD works

BillboardBD is a marketplace for renting outdoor billboard advertising space in Dhaka,
Bangladesh. Three kinds of people use it: advertisers who want space, billboard owners
who have space to rent, and the BillboardBD team who review everything in between.

Note on figures: this guide describes the rules, never the numbers behind them. The
commission rate, the advance percentage, how long a hold lasts, the final payment window
and the board listing fee are all settings the BillboardBD team can change. Always check
the current values rather than assuming.

## The three roles

An **advertiser** (also called a client) searches billboards on the map, picks dates,
pays an advance to submit a request, uploads their artwork, and pays the balance once
the request is approved.

A **billboard owner** lists their boards, pays a one-time listing fee per board, keeps
permits up to date, accepts or declines booking requests, installs the advertisement,
uploads photographic proof, and receives payouts.

The **BillboardBD team** (admin) reviews every booking request, checks board listings and
permits before they go public, verifies proof of installation, and sends owner payouts.

## Why every booking is reviewed twice

A booking request has to clear two separate approvals: the BillboardBD team checks the
request and the board's permit paperwork, and then the billboard owner accepts it.

Both exist for a reason. The team's review catches expired permits and requests that
break advertising rules before an owner wastes time on them. The owner's acceptance
exists because it is their board — nobody can sell space on it without their agreement.

Either party declining ends the booking and refunds the advertiser automatically.

## Currency and payments

All prices and payments are in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT). Card and mobile payments are
handled by SSLCommerz, the payment gateway; BillboardBD never stores card details.

## Getting help

Questions about a specific booking, board or payment are best asked with its reference
number to hand. The assistant can look up anything belonging to the account you are
signed in with, but it cannot see anyone else's records, and it cannot make or change a
booking, accept a request or move money — those actions live on their own pages.
