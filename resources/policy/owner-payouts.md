# Earnings and payouts for billboard owners

The single most common owner question is some version of "I know a booking was paid, so
why has my figure not moved?" The answer is almost always that the money is real but has
not reached the last stage yet. Earned money and received money are different things.

## The four stages money passes through

Every taka an owner has earned sits in exactly one of four buckets, and they always add
back up to total earnings.

**In progress** — earned, but nothing is with the BillboardBD team yet. Either the
advertiser still owes the balance, or the owner has not uploaded proof of installation.
Nobody else is holding this up.

**Awaiting verification** — the owner has uploaded proof and the team has not checked it
yet. This is the only bucket where the team is the hold-up.

**Ready for payout** — the team has verified the installation. The money is payable on
the next payout run and shows as the outstanding balance on the Payouts page.

**Paid out** — actually sent. This bucket always equals the payout history exactly,
because a payout is the only thing that fills it.

## When each payment counts as earned

The advance an advertiser pays does not count as owner earnings straight away. Until a
booking has cleared both the team's review and the owner's own acceptance, that advance
can still be refunded, so it is not the owner's money yet.

The balance is different. It can only be paid after both approvals are in, so there is no
rejection left that could claw it back — it counts the moment it is paid.

## Commission

BillboardBD takes a percentage of what a booking collects. The rate is set by the team
and is frozen onto each booking when its advance is paid, so a change to the platform
rate never moves the value of a booking already sold.

The commission follows the cash: it applies to what has actually been collected on a
booking, not to the whole contract value.

## The listing fee is not an expense against earnings

The one-time fee an owner pays to list a board is money paid to the platform, not money a
board earned. It does not appear in earnings figures and is not deducted from them.

## Payout runs

Payouts are issued by the BillboardBD team. A payout covers everything currently verified
and ready, and produces a receipt with a payment reference.

Payout details — method, account name, account number, bank and branch — are set by the
owner on their own account, and should be correct before a run.

## Why proof of installation is the gate

No verification, no payout. The rule exists because BillboardBD guarantees advertisers
that a campaign they paid for is actually up. Verified photographic proof is what makes
that promise checkable, so it is also the point at which the owner's money is released.
