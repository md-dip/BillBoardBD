# Booking a billboard, stage by stage

An advertiser's booking passes through seven stages. Knowing which one a booking is at
tells you exactly whose desk it is on and what happens next.

## Searching and comparing boards

Billboards can be filtered by area, structure type, size and price, or by distance from a
point on the map. Only boards the BillboardBD team has approved appear publicly; a board
still in review is invisible until it clears.

Boards are priced either per day or per month, and each board is one or the other — not
both. A board's listing also shows when its advertising permit expires.

## Holding your dates

Choosing a start and end date places a hold. The hold locks those dates to you for a
short period while you finish the request, so nobody else can take them from under you
mid-checkout.

A hold is not a booking. If it runs out before the request is completed, the dates are
released back to everyone else and nothing is charged. The dates can simply be picked
again if they are still free.

## Submitting the campaign

Before the request goes anywhere, the advertiser adds the campaign details — brand name,
advertising category, a description, and the artwork file that will be printed and
installed.

An advance payment is required to submit the request. This is a percentage of the booking
total, and it is what turns a held slot into a real request queued for review.

## Admin review

The BillboardBD team checks the request and the board's permit status. This is usually
quick. If the team declines the request, the advance is refunded in full, automatically —
no claim needs to be made.

## Owner acceptance

The billboard owner then accepts or declines. If they decline, the advance is refunded in
full, automatically, exactly as with an admin rejection.

When the owner accepts, the booking is confirmed and a due date is set for the remaining
balance.

## Paying the balance

The rest of the booking total is due before the due date set at confirmation. Once it is
paid, the booking is paid in full and moves on to installation.

## Installation and proof

The owner installs the advertisement and uploads a photograph of it in place. The
BillboardBD team verifies that photograph against the campaign. Once verified, the
booking becomes active — the campaign is live and running.

## What each stage name means

- **held** — dates are locked while the request is completed. Nothing paid yet.
- **pending_admin_review** — advance paid, waiting on the BillboardBD team.
- **pending_owner_approval** — team approved, waiting on the billboard owner.
- **confirmed** — both approvals in. The balance is now due.
- **paid_in_full** — fully paid, waiting for the owner to install and upload proof.
- **pending_proof_review** — proof uploaded, waiting on the team to verify it.
- **active** — verified. The campaign is live.
- **rejected** — declined at one of the two review points. Advance refunded.
