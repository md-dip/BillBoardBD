# Listing a billboard and keeping it legal

## Submitting a board

An owner adds a board with its location, size, structure type and price, then uploads a
photograph of it and its advertising permit document. A one-time listing fee is payable
per board, set by the BillboardBD team.

A board is not public the moment it is submitted. It goes into a review queue and only
appears on the map once the team approves it.

## What the listing review checks

The team checks that the board exists where it says it does, that the photograph is of
that board, and that the permit document is genuine and current. Boards whose paperwork
does not hold up are rejected with a reason attached, so the owner knows what to fix.

## If a listing is rejected

The listing fee is refunded in full, automatically, when the team rejects a listing. An
owner is never charged for a board that was not allowed to go live.

A rejected board can be corrected and resubmitted.

## Listing statuses

- **pending_payment** — submitted, the listing fee has not gone through yet.
- **pending_review** — paid, sitting in the team's review queue.
- **approved** — live and visible on the public map.
- **rejected** — declined, with a reason. The listing fee was refunded.

## Advertising permits

Every board carries an advertising permit with an expiry date. The permit is the owner's
responsibility to renew, not BillboardBD's.

Permits matter beyond paperwork: the team checks permit status during booking review, so
a board with an expired permit will see its booking requests declined. Keeping the expiry
date current on the listing is what prevents that.

## Accepting and declining booking requests

A booking request reaching an owner has already passed the team's review and has a paid
advance behind it. The owner accepts or declines it.

Declining refunds the advertiser's advance automatically and in full. There is no penalty
for declining, but a board that repeatedly declines requests earns less.

Accepting confirms the booking and starts the advertiser's balance payment window.

## Installing and proving it

After the advertiser has paid in full, the owner installs the advertisement and uploads a
photograph of it in place. This proof is what the BillboardBD team verifies, and
verification is the gate money must pass before it can be paid out.

An owner who has not uploaded proof has earned the money but cannot yet be paid it.
